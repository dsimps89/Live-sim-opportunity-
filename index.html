09 / What the atlas means

The largest source tier is C$20/task with 1,908 inferred links (31.5%); C$20–C$150 tiers total 4,172 links (68.9%). These are heuristic business-task pairings, not actual sales or market demand. 140 task concepts span 14 value tiers. The 516 NOC classification codes are a separate classification layer; no verified NOC-to-business matching is implied. Household feasibility, verified need, full cost, safety and repeatable delivery should guide any real-world decision. The Canadian issuer terrain is historical financial context, not evidence of local household revenue.
